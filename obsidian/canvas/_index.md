# Canvases, one per article

Obsidian's graph view is force-directed, so it cannot draw a hierarchy. These can: each canvas places the article's claims at the level the annotator drew them, question at the top through concessions at the bottom. Every card is the claim's own note.

Cards are coloured by Toulmin family. Edge labels carry the sign (**+1** backs, **-1** pushes back, **0** neither) and the relation; a relation suffixed `~` was recovered from the map's invisible `~~~` operator and typed by convention, which is most of the question-to-prior and prior-to-evidence structure.
