## How to read the score

- A **slot** call is right when the prediction and the article agree on whether that slot appears at all. Slots marked *unsure* or *no such slot yet* are not counted.
- Most slots appear in nearly every article, so saying 'yes' to all of them already scores well. Those are the **free** ones. The **real calls** are the slots the article leaves empty — only a prediction that actually says 'no' can get them.
- **Content** is judged box by box by the reader of the article: *right* = the same claim; *close* = the right slot but a different claim, detail or kind; *opposite* = the content contradicts the prediction.
- **New slots** are not failures of the prediction. The framework had no word for them yet — that is a finding about the framework.
