# Ray Dalio Source Audit

**Project:** Understanding the Logic of Influential Thinkers  
**Research goal:** Identify first-party materials that can support extraction and evaluation of Ray Dalio's reasoning patterns.  
**Audit date:** September 2026

## Key conclusion

Ray Dalio is a promising pilot subject because his public materials cover multiple topics and time periods. No single platform is sufficient:

- **Substack** provides recent, clean, downloadable long-form posts, but its archive only begins in February 2026.
- **LinkedIn Articles** extends the visible history back to at least September 2013, but it has no native article-download feature and some entries are mainly videos.
- **Bridgewater Research & Insights** provides valuable historical and quantitative material, but its agreement and PDF disclosures restrict copying and redistribution. These files should remain internal unless permission is obtained.

## Reviewed sample articles

### 1. The Tribute System: The New World Order

- **Date:** June 18, 2026
- **Platform:** Substack
- **Topic:** Geopolitics and China
- **URL:** https://substack.com/@raydalio/p-202634008
- **PDF download:** https://raydalio.substack.com/api/v1/post/pdf?postId=202634008
- **PDF filename:** `Principled Perspectives - The Tribute System_ The New World Order.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** Changing US-China power conditions and the behavior of Asian leaders.
- **Reasoning:** Interprets current events through historical power transitions, Chinese history, the tribute system, and the Art of War.
- **Outcome:** Predicts that China will increasingly pressure Taiwan and US allies, often without direct conflict.
- **Research value:** Tests Dalio's use of historical analogy and causal prediction outside portfolio decisions.

### 2. Investment Principles: What Should You Do Under Existing Conditions?

- **Date:** June 15, 2026
- **Platform:** Substack
- **Topic:** Investment strategy
- **URL:** https://substack.com/@raydalio/p-202146486
- **PDF download:** https://raydalio.substack.com/api/v1/post/pdf?postId=202146486
- **PDF filename:** `Principled Perspectives - Investment Principles_ What Should You Do Under Existing Conditions_.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** AI-led market concentration, expensive assets, and high uncertainty.
- **Reasoning:** Separates the quality of a technology from the attractiveness of its stocks and emphasizes uncertainty, correlation, and risk-adjusted return.
- **Outcome:** Recommends diversified, high-confidence, uncorrelated bets rather than concentrated exposure.
- **Research value:** A recent statement of Dalio's portfolio decision principles.

### 3. The Big Dangers of Big Bubbles with Big Wealth Gaps

- **Date:** February 19, 2026
- **Platform:** Substack
- **Topic:** Bubbles, inequality, and political risk
- **URL:** https://substack.com/@raydalio/p-188503778
- **PDF download:** https://raydalio.substack.com/api/v1/post/pdf?postId=188503778
- **PDF filename:** `Principled Perspectives - The Big Dangers of Big Bubbles with Big Wealth Gaps.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** Large financial wealth relative to cash, asset bubbles, and large wealth gaps.
- **Reasoning:** Explains a liquidity mechanism in which taxes or other cash needs can force asset sales and reverse self-reinforcing price dynamics.
- **Outcome:** Argues that wealth-tax pressure could help prick a bubble and produce financial, social, and political effects.
- **Research value:** Contains an explicit causal chain connecting finance and politics.

### 4. My AI Clone and What We Can Expect from AI Clones

- **Date:** February 19, 2026
- **Platform:** Substack
- **Topic:** Artificial intelligence and digital identity
- **URL:** https://substack.com/@raydalio/p-188492400
- **PDF download:** https://raydalio.substack.com/api/v1/post/pdf?postId=188492400
- **PDF filename:** `Principled Perspectives - My AI Clone and What We Can Expect from AI Clones.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** Generic LLMs do not encode a particular person's values, preferences, principles, and judgment.
- **Reasoning:** Uses decades of recorded decision rules, curated questions and answers, human-AI complementarity, and internal testing to argue for personalization.
- **Outcome:** Predicts that personalized AI clones will become powerful decision partners.
- **Research value:** Directly overlaps with the capstone's goal of representing a person's reasoning.
- **Caution:** The reported 95% and 80% performance figures are Dalio's self-reported claims, not independently verified results.

### 5. Stock Market Bubble?

- **Date:** February 2, 2021
- **Platform:** Bridgewater
- **Topic:** Market bubbles
- **URL:** https://www.bridgewater.com/research-and-insights/ray-dalio-stock-market-bubble
- **PDF download:** https://www.bridgewater.com/_document/stock-market-bubble?id=00000177-ba4b-d5a2-ab7f-fb7b9efe0000
- **PDF filename:** `stock-market-bubble.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** Valuation, sustainability, new buyers, sentiment, leverage, and forward purchases.
- **Reasoning:** Combines six quantitative gauges and compares 2021 conditions with the bubbles of 1929 and 2000.
- **Outcome:** Finds extreme bubbles in some emerging-technology stocks, but not in the overall US market. It favors non-bubble stocks while warning that market timing is imprecise.
- **Research value:** Provides a clear systematic and quantitative framework for comparison with his 2026 investment views.

### 6. The Fourth Big Force: Climate Change

- **Date:** December 21, 2023
- **Platform:** Bridgewater
- **Topic:** Climate finance
- **URL:** https://www.bridgewater.com/research-and-insights/the-fourth-big-force-climate-change
- **PDF download:** https://www.bridgewater.com/_document/the-fourth-big-force-climate-change?id=00000195-16f8-d139-af9d-3efb4d970000
- **PDF filename:** `the-fourth-big-force-dec-2023.pdf`
- **Input/observation:** Temperature, emissions, catastrophe losses, climate-finance flows, and unequal national resources.
- **Reasoning:** Connects physical trends with incentives, financing constraints, differences between countries, and Dalio's five-big-forces framework.
- **Outcome:** Concludes that mitigation targets are unlikely to be met and predicts greater emphasis on adaptation and profitable innovation.
- **Research value:** Shows similar causal reasoning outside traditional investment topics.
- **Date note:** The article date is December 21, 2023, even though the downloaded PDF file was generated later.

## Preliminary reasoning pattern

Across the six samples, a recurring structure is:

1. **Define observable conditions.**
2. **Identify cause-and-effect relationships.**
3. **Compare the present with a historical or conceptual framework.**
4. **Acknowledge uncertainty or limitations.**
5. **Produce a prediction or recommendation.**
